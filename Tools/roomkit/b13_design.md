# Batch 13 - sixteen SCPs in six rooms (working notes)

Source facts (from the SCP wiki articles, CC BY-SA 3.0; SCP-1337 from the 2019 archived page, the live one is a placeholder).

| SCP | Class | What it is | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 019 | Keter | Greek ceramic vase, 1.8 m mouth, 2.4 m high, unbreakable | small hostile humanoids (019-2) climb out, ~1/hour at 0 C and at rest; faster when moved/threatened/hot. Flammable. Kept on a grate in a 3x3x4 m concrete room with an incinerator and an observation window |
| 247 | Euclid | adult female Bengal tiger | everyone sees a harmless striped kitten and wants to pet it. 20x40 m zoo-plan enclosure lined with SCP-148; fed in a separate enclosure |
| 249 | Euclid | windowless door, faded white paint, fixed to a wall | opens onto a random door within 850 m; about every 500 uses onto a random place anywhere. Takes the look of the door it connects to. Access unrestricted, log kept |
| 354 | Keter | pool of red liquid, no definite banks, no known bottom | hostile creatures emerge periodically. Area-354 is built around it |
| 377 | Safe | box of La Choy fortune cookies, restocks every 12 h | fortunes are specific to the opener and always come true, not always good. Kept in the break room, third cabinet left of the fridge; one cookie per 48 h |
| 978 | Safe | red-and-black Polaroid Supercolor 645 CL | photo shows what the subject wanted to be doing. Locker H-J-12 |
| 1337 | Safe -> Euclid | wraith of Mary Talish: blonde, 150 cm, red mid-calf skirt, buttoned shirt, red sweater (1337-Gamma) | hitch-hikes on the 19th, vanishes at the graveyard leaving the sweater; whoever touches the sweater wants to return it to her parents; sweater vanishes from containment at sunset |
| 1762 | Safe (Neutralized) | cardboard box 32x20x26 cm sprayed silver, "HERE BE DRAGONS" in marker on the lid | puffs black smoke, ~20 s later origami dragons (9-30 cm, kami paper) fly out in a flock and play with people. Harmless, must not leave the unit |
| 1983 | Keter | front door of an abandoned Wyoming farmhouse | doorway is a lightless spatial anomaly; shadow bipeds (1983-2) come out and take hearts; only silver bullets fired by believers hurt them. Agent's last letter found inside |
| 3038 | Euclid | white wooden door, ornate Victorian brass knob, keyhole no light passes | nobody has ever opened it; unease within 5 m; staff near it lose memory, obsess, file endless proposals instead of results. Glass antechamber, keycard, handwritten reports only |
| 3041 | Safe | 12 cm iron knife, braided leather hilt, rust-coloured, smells of spoiled meat | hold it long enough and you later dream of using it on strangers in the place you held it. Secure locker, no bare skin |
| 3045 | Safe | bzzip.exe on an isolated PC | "compresses" text/video by rewriting it simpler each pass until almost nothing is left (Hamlet test log). PC destroyed after tests |
| 3046 | Safe | 5.2 cm spiked purple toy ball | immovable until touched; then sticks to skin and grows without limit; 5-15 min later the nearest phone rings: a nasal New England voice offers to detach it for ~$500 of anything, or for labour. 60 cm locker |
| 3056 | Euclid | H7N7 flu strain ("Fireflu") | struvite crystals in the nose + methane in the lungs: every sneeze is a small burst of flame, harmless to the host. Sample in a biohazard locker; carriers kept in a biohazard chamber |
| 4666 | Keter | very tall (2-2.3 m), emaciated, naked old man | 21 Dec - 1 Jan: watches isolated family homes for nights, then kills; leaves crude toys. Uncontained |
| 5094 | Safe | "Miss J", a 2D cut-out teacher in a 1999 kids' CD-ROM | sapient, kind, only talks about teaching; lessons run 30 hours with breaks; always starts with letters, colours, numbers. 37 copies in low-risk storage |
