==========================================================================
 SCP: Containment Breach - Indev
 Changelog - v0.1
==========================================================================

 An "Indev" prototype build of SCP: Containment Breach. The facility has a
 smaller SCP roster with more depth, lots of new anomalous items, and new
 player systems and HUD.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. ROSTER AND FACILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Core SCP roster
  - The facility's core SCPs are now SCP-173, SCP-106, SCP-079, SCP-895,
    SCP-914, SCP-513, SCP-990 (secret), SCP-420-J and SCP-789-J.
  - Removed from generation: SCP-008, 012, 035, 049, 066, 205, 294, 372,
    682, 860, 939, 966, 970, 1048, 1123, 1162, 1499, 714, 1025, 148 and 427.
    Their rooms and events are commented out and tagged [INDEV CUT].
  - SCP-096 is no longer a room of its own. It appears only through the
    SCP-096 photo document (see below).
  - SCP-500 is back in the Medical Bay.
  - Restored: SCP-008 (its room and the Medical Bay 008-1 zombie event),
    SCP-012, SCP-035, SCP-1499 and SCP-500 (room2scps2) and SCP-714,
    SCP-1025 and SCP-860 (room2scps). All were re-added on their old
    rooms with a very high placement priority so they always fit.

 Map
  - The map is now 24 wide and 32 tall, to fit the new Indev Zone.
  - Key rooms have a very high placement priority, so they always fit.
  - Zones were reorganised:
      - Medical Bay: Light Containment Zone
      - room2ccont, room2offices4: Heavy Containment Zone
      - Reception (room4info): Entrance Zone
      - SCP-895 and SCP-513 rooms: Light Containment Zone
      - The security room (room2sl) is back in the Light Containment
        Zone, where it is in the original game. It had been moved to the
        Heavy Containment Zone for a while.
  - room3servers2 was cut.
  - The Prototype Room (roompj) is now placed in one Heavy Containment
    Zone dead end every run. SCP-718 lives there.
  - Test rooms testroom3x3 and testroom4x4 are forced into the Light
    Containment Zone. The zone now always gets at least two 4-way
    crossroads for them.
  - The Light Containment to Heavy Containment checkpoint starts locked
    down again, as in the original game. Pull the lever in the security
    room (room2sl, Level 3 door) to lift the lockdown; the checkpoint
    doors then open with a Level 3 keycard. While the security room sat
    in the Heavy Containment Zone the lockdown had been switched off,
    because the lever was behind the checkpoint it unlocks.

 Indev Zone (new)
  - A new fourth zone between the Heavy Containment Zone and the Entrance
    Zone. The route is now Light Containment -> Heavy Containment ->
    Indev Zone -> Entrance Zone.
  - Filler rooms: room2indevclosets, room2indevclosets1, room3indevtunnter
    and room4indevtunnter. Dead ends and corners there use Heavy
    Containment rooms.
  - room2checkpointindev: a new checkpoint between the Heavy Containment
    Zone and the Indev Zone, with two Level 4 keycard doors.
  - For doors and ambience, the Indev Zone counts as Heavy Containment.
  - Saves from before this update may load incorrectly. Start a new game.

 Facility generation
  - 5 starting layout styles, picked per seed.
  - Rare loops: circular corridors, alternate routes and shortcuts in the
    Heavy Containment Zone.
  - Layout validation. Maps are regenerated until every room can be
    reached and there are no large enclosed empty areas.
  - Broken sections: a few doors are jammed shut for good, but only where
    another route exists.
  - Filler 4-way rooms can face any direction.
  - Debug report for rooms that can't be reached.

 SCP-914 chamber
  - The SCP-914 chamber door now has a keypad instead of a keycard slot.
  - The code is on a new note found in the storage closets (room2closets).
  - SCP-914 now refines every item. Items with no hand-written recipe get
    the generic treatment:
      Rough     - broken down to raw substance: dust on the floor, item
                  gone
      Coarse    - stripped down. Comes out as "Worn", charge or uses cut
                  in half
      1:1       - reworked into another item of the same kind, if one
                  exists (same "group" in Data\items.ini)
      Fine      - one grade purer: plain -> "Refined" -> "Pure". Charge
                  or uses x1.5, up to 100
      Very Fine - straight to "Pure" (full charge), but 1 in 5 goes past
                  the point of holding together and comes out as powder
  - Grade names (Worn/Refined/Pure) are added to the item's own display
    name and are saved next to the .cbsav file, so the grade survives
    quick-loading and normal loading.
  - Recipes for specific items can be added to a new Data\refine914.ini
    file, checked before the built-in recipes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. CORE SCP REWORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

 SCP-173
  - New model and texture: a rougher, cracked concrete sculpture with
    painted features.
  - Random starting position.
  - You may catch sightings of it in the distance.

 SCP-079 (the facility AI)
  - Plays with the lights, doors, cameras and alarms.
  - Tells half-truths over the intercom.

 SCP-106
  - After an encounter it may corrode doors open or seal rooms shut.
  - Sealed doors are unsealed before saving.

 SCP-513
  - A phantom bell you may or may not have heard.

 Old Bill (SCP-513-1)
  - Every 1-3 minutes (2-4 minutes after an encounter) a cowbell clangs
    over the intercom.
  - A few seconds later Old Bill appears behind you and stalks you along
    the path you walked. He creeps while watched and moves fast when you
    look away.
  - Stare at him for 4 seconds and he fades. If he catches you, you get
    hurt and he vanishes. Otherwise he gives up after about 2 minutes.
  - Console: "oldbill" rings the cowbell right away.

 SCP-990 (secret)
  - A very rare dream while you rest by holding the blink key.
  - The dream area is picked at random each run.

 3. NEW SCPs IN ROOMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

 SCP-718 - Eye on a stalk (Prototype Room)
  - Stay in its sight and paranoia builds in stages until you smash it.
  - Its fluid causes blisters. A copy of the eye grows on the floor, or
    on you.
  - A first aid kit can cut the copy out, and it hurts.
  - Hazmat suits, MJOLNIR armour and CF-1 glasses all protect against it.

 SCP-719 - 1975 GE Porta-Color TV (room2offices4)
  - Click it to switch it on or off.
  - Watch it too long and the broadcast drifts toward a burning, sun-
    worshipping channel while the radiation doubles.
  - Switching it off resets it. Leaving it on makes it overload.

 SCP-993 - "Bobble the Clown" TV (room2offices2)
  - Look at the screen and Bobble's episode starts. You get a stabbing
    headache and black out for the whole show.
  - SCP-173 plays fair while you're out.
  - The TV has a power switch: click it while it shows static.
  - CF-1 glasses reduce the episode to a headache.

 SCP-738 - "The Devil's Deal" (dead-end room)
  - A desk, a chair and a throne. Sit in the chair and a voice makes you
    offers.
  - Controls: [1-5] make a request, [B] bargain, [Y] accept, [N] refuse.
  - Deals are fulfilled to the letter, and the price is always paid.
  - Three deals per run.

 SCP-330 - "Take Only Two" (cafeteria)
  - A bowl of candy on a table. Click it to take a piece.
  - The count resets after 10 minutes.
  - Take a third piece and you lose both hands:
      - heavy bleeding;
      - you drop everything you're holding;
      - you can't pick anything up except first aid, SCP-9282 and
        SCP-8228.
  - SCP-9282 or SCP-8228 grows the hands back.
  - Candy relieves stress.
  - Console: "heal" restores your hands. "restore330", "scp330" or
    "reset330" restores the bowl.

 SCP-122 - Star night light (Medical Bay)
  - While it's on in the same room: stress melts away, new stress barely
    builds, sanity recovers and paranoia fades.
  - Click it to unplug it or plug it back in.
  - It flickers now and then, and loses power during outages.

 SCP-1074 - "Out of the Painting" (room2scp1074room)
  - A painting of a woman on a beach. Stare at it for about a second and
    it pulls you toward it, while its music plays.
  - Look away long enough and it lets go. Get too close and you're locked
    in place while it talks to you.
  - Stare too long and you die of catatonia.
  - Afterwards the painting shows behind your eyelids when you blink for
    3 minutes.
  - A fake Class-D may appear in a doorway, then vanish when you get close
    or blink.
  - Leaving the room releases you at any stage. If it can't reach you
    within 20 seconds, it gives up.

 SCP-109 - "The Infinite Canteen" (room1newscproom)
  - A WW2 army canteen that never runs dry.
  - Drinking refills stamina, clears blur, heals a little, slows bleeding,
    lowers stress and flushes some radiation.
  - Drink again within 12 seconds and water gets into your lungs. The 4th
    quick drink drowns you.
  - Can't be used with a gas mask or hazmat suit.
  - SCP-914: Rough bursts it. Fine/Very Fine frosts it over.

 SCP-963 - "Immortality" (room1newscproom)
  - Carry it and the next death is taken by the mind inside it: you get back up,
    healed. It needs 2 minutes between saves.
  - Two saves work. The third death is final, and the amulet keeps the body.
  - Using it shows how much of you is still you.
  - SCP-914 does nothing to it.

 SCP-2295 - "The Bear with a Heart of Patchwork" (room1newscproom)
  - A patchwork teddy bear in an open toy chest.
  - Use it while hurt or bleeding and it stitches felt into your wounds:
    injuries and bleeding fully healed. It then needs 4 minutes.
  - Otherwise it hugs you, which lowers stress.
  - SCP-914: Rough/Coarse tears it (10 minutes to repair). Fine/Very Fine
    restuffs it, ready at once.

 SCP-005 - Skeleton Key (room2newoffices2)
  - Select it like a keycard: every card reader treats it as Level 6.
  - While held, keypad doors within reach unlock for good.
  - Doors locked down by the facility still stay shut.

 SCP-1471 - "MalO ver1.0.0" phone (room2newoffices2)
  - Use the phone and the app installs itself. It can't be uninstalled.
  - Every minute or two a black-furred figure with a dog's skull stands
    behind you, out of sight. Turn and see it and it vanishes. It raises
    stress but never hurts you.
  - The installed app is kept in your save.
  - SCP-914 Rough/Coarse destroys the phone and ends it.

 SCP-268 - "Cap of Neglect" (room2newroomtest6, Chamber T6)
  - A brown felt fez with a black tassel, on a plinth behind a keypad
    door. Put it on from the inventory.
  - While it is worn nobody registers you: security cameras never confirm
    a sighting, so the Mobile Task Force is never told where you are, and
    being unobserved lets your stress bleed away.
  - It does NOT work on SCP-173, which reacts to being looked at rather
    than to being remembered.
  - The cost is time, and it never carries over between uses:
      2:00  the edges go soft, and the fez no longer comes off on the
            first try - you have to hold on to it
      3:00  you lose your own name
      4:10  you are forgotten entirely, and that is the end of the run
  - Taking it off clears everything at once.
  - Whether it is on is kept in your save.
  - SCP-914 Rough/Coarse tear the felt apart. Everything finer hands it
    back working exactly the same.
  - New model, texture, icon, document and note, and a new steel display
    plinth prop, all generated.

 SCP-458 - "The Never-Ending Pizza Box" (room2newcaferoom)
  - A Foundation cafeteria pizza box on a table. Use it to eat a slice of
    your favourite pizza.
  - Each slice refills stamina, lowers stress, heals a little and slows
    bleeding.
  - More than 3 slices within a minute: you feel sick and the box stays
    empty for 2 minutes.
  - SCP-914: Rough/Coarse squashes it. Fine/Very Fine adds extra toppings
    (the next slice heals much more).

 3b. NEW EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Every event is saved with your game, and stops (NPCs stand still,
 timers freeze) when you leave its room.

 "Sublevel Blackout" (room2newroom1)
  - Dr. Harlan and a Class-D, Rodriguez, walk in and talk to you.
  - Rodriguez goes down the ramp to reset a breaker. The lights die, he
    screams and is gone, and blood is all that's left.
  - The generator brings the lights back and Harlan runs.
  - Sublevel items: a maintenance log and a first aid kit.

 "Remote Detonation" (room2newroom2)
  - Dr. Voss and Miller (a Class-D) meet you: both warheads were armed
    before the lockdown.
  - Take the elevator upstairs, where SCP-079 greets you on the intercom.
  - Pull the ALPHA and OMEGA levers down to disarm the warheads.
  - Come back down and they thank you and leave.
  - Upstairs: the detonation log and a Level 3 keycard.

 "Omega Silo" (room2newroom8)
  - Dr. Okafor explains that SCP-079 started a silo launch.
  - Upstairs a 3:00 countdown starts, with an alarm, flickering lights and
    an on-screen timer.
  - Pike, a Class-D hiding in the silo hall, points the way.
  - Close the red fuel valves on both tanks, then pull the ABORT lever on
    the terminal wall. The lever stays locked until both valves are shut.
  - Open tanks vent scalding steam every few seconds. SCP-079 taunts you
    as the clock runs down.
  - Abort: the alarm stops and a Level 4 keycard drops. Fail: you die
    upstairs, or take heavy injuries downstairs.
  - Document: Omega Silo Launch Log.

 "Dead Circuit" (room2newroom65)
  - Dr. Reyes waits upstairs and tells you about the Hollow, a pitch-black
    figure that only moves while nobody is looking.
  - Pull three breaker levers on the lower level (west records room,
    south wall, north-east wall).
  - The Hollow closes in when you look away. Stare at it and it flinches
    away. Within reach it breathes on you first, which gives you a second
    to turn around.
  - A catch drains stamina and raises stress. The third catch kills you.
  - Technician Hale (upstairs) and Tobias (a Class-D on the lower level)
    give hints and tell you where the next breaker is.
  - All three breakers on: the generator starts, the Hollow burns away,
    and Reyes gives you her night vision goggles.
  - Items: Lower Level Night Shift Notes and a first aid kit.

      SCP-280   kept in the dark inside the block. Inside the cell you see

 4h. TEN ROOMS, TEN MORE SCPs (2026-10-02, seventh batch)

 Ten generated rooms, each placed once per map, for SCP-052, 024, 482,
 953, 1269, 1440, 2701, 3325, 4258 and 6096. Built by
 Tools\roomkit\gen_b7.py; game code in IndevS10.bb and the generated
 IndevS10Data.bb. New models (GFX\npcs): the R4 subway car and its door
 leaves, a nine-tailed fox, a kennel dog, the sheet, the mailbox, an
 SCP-3325-1 with a moving jaw, a studio guardian, a liver cart, the
 straitjacket. New sounds (SFX\Indev\*.wav, synthesised): the train,
 barking, applause, buzzer, jingle, confined wind, puppet voices, a

 5g. LOADING TIMES, FPS AND STABILITY (2026-11-12)

  Measured with the room test (new PROF lines in RoomTest\log.txt; startup
  stages in RoomTest\boot.txt) before and after every change. Launch to
  playable: about 28 s -> 16-17 s (intro videos skipped).
  Map generation, seeds, room placement and connectivity are untouched.
  - NPC navigation (InitWayPoints): ~6.1 s -> ~0.8 s.
    * Every waypoint link got its own debug line mesh - about 8,000 mesh
      entities, only visible in wireframe mode, built at load and carried
      through every frame. Now only with the -waylines launch flag. This
      also helps FPS.
    * Links are found with a 7-unit grid instead of comparing every
      waypoint with every other, and the line-of-sight tests are cast with
      only the nearby rooms visible (a far room cannot block a ray shorter
      than 7 units). Links are filled in the original order.
  - Room textures: the texture/material cache was searched name by name
    (three full scans per mesh surface) and was case-sensitive, so e.g.
    Pipes.jpg was decoded 37 times. It now has a hash index and ignores
    case: 1,015 texture decodes -> 792. Surfaces with no texture no longer
    try to load one.
  - Footstep sounds: the per-step material lookup uses the same index (it
    scanned ~800 materials up to three times per footstep), and mixed-case
    textures now get their surface's footstep sound (they never matched
    before).
  - Lightmaps: 35 room lightmaps were 2048x2048 (~21 MB of video memory
    each). Downscaled to 1024 - softer by a hair, a quarter of the memory
    (~550 MB less), faster to load. Originals kept in
    GFX\map\lightmaps_2048_backup\.
  - INI files: GetINIString read the cached file from the top, a character
    at a time, for every single key. Files are now indexed by section once
    (first section of a name wins, as before) and read in one block.
    LoadEntities: ~5.2 s -> ~3.5 s.
  - Loading screen: redrawn at most ~30 times a second instead of hundreds
    of full redraws while the map is built.
  - Startup: the intro videos (2-7 s) can be skipped as a whole with any
    key or mouse click (a key used to skip only the current video); the
    "play startup video" option and -novid still turn them off.
  - Fix: a missed InitWayPoints quirk - once one pair passed the height
    check, it stayed passed for the rest of the list; now checked per pair.
  - Stability: checked on several seeds - all 225 forced rooms placed, no
    crashes; FPS in the newest rooms 170-305 (was 154-275 for the same
    views).

 4a. NEW SCPs (2026-09-23 batch)

 SCP-2935 - "O, Death" (room1scp2935, Indev Zone)
  - Reworked into a phased single-room sequence instead of one static
    room: discovery -> static -> emergency broadcast -> space-time
    transition -> dead-world silence -> distress log -> lockdown.
  - Phase 1 (discovery): entering the room starts the radio on its own.
  - Phase 2 (static): ~4s of static with a screen-shake pulse.
  - Phase 3 (broadcast): staged Msg lines carry the actual Foundation
    emergency broadcast text, timed like a real announcement.
  - Phase 4 (transition): fog thickens briefly for the "crossing" beat,
    then clears.
  - Phase 5 (active): the room becomes the dead-world reality - near-
    silent ambience, rare (1%/sec) stingers (static/distant radio/wind),
    no monster, no NPCs. IndevStress ticks slowly the whole time.
  - Phase 6 (distress log): reading doc2935c (the Site-81 distress ping
    document) triggers the full 0313-0500 timestamp log to print one
    line at a time, straight from the source material's timestamps.
  - Phase 7 (lockdown): alarm blip, camera shake, "[LOCKDOWN COMPLETE]",
    then permanent silence - the last beat of the sequence.
  - Three new evidence items scattered on the floor: item2935paper (the
    Hendricks County Flyer, dated Apr 19 2016), item2935phone (the dead
    Samsung Note 5, last message about card night), item2935calendar
    (the Site-81 desk calendar, every square crossed off through Apr 19
    and nothing after). Each has its own Use-time flavour text pulled
    directly from the recovered-artifacts log.
  - Four full documents remain for players who want the complete files:
    doc2935 (main SCP file), doc2935b (artifacts log), doc2935c
    (distress pings - also the phase-6 trigger), doc2935d (the "You're
    Death, too." audio transcript).
  - Vanilla-first: built entirely from existing assets/functions
    (StreamSound_Strict, PlaySound_Strict, CameraFogMode, CameraShake,
    IndevStress, CreateItem). No new rooms or meshes were needed for
    this pass - the full "cemetery -> road -> house -> Site-81 ->
    Site-19" exploration chain from the design doc would need new
    room meshes that don't exist yet in this build.

 SCP-2536 - "Big Xmas" (permanent chamber + December office event)
  - New dedicated room: room1scp2536 in the Indev Zone. A shape-1 endroom
    that always contains the tree + wrapped present, regardless of the

    present (SCP-2536-1) sits in front of it.
  - Walk onto the present to open it. The gift picker matches your
    current need: heavy injuries -> firstaid, high infection -> SCP-500,
    high stress -> SCP-420-J, otherwise a Level 4 keycard (opens the
    SCP-770 chamber if you haven't finished the 173-B practice yet).
  - Foundation guidance says do NOT accept. If you leave the room

  - Stress ticks up slightly while you stand near it. Vanilla-first:

 SCP-770 - "Radiosynthetic Mould" (room1scp770, Indev Zone)
  - A small iron-56 flask on the observation desk of a locked Keter cell,
    gated behind an old-Indev door on a Level 4 keycard reader. The Level 4
    key drops from SCP-173-B's practice-session reward, so the chain is
    173-B -> Level 4 -> SCP-770 flask -> 914 very fine -> Level 5.
  - Sealed in inventory: does nothing (dead weight). Right-click Use to
    unscrew the stopper - a growing patch of radiosynthetic mould pours
    onto the floor at your feet, glows white-hot, spreads through spores,
    burns your shoes, and irradiates the room.

    Injuries/Bloodloss/Infect. Do not linger.
  - SCP-914 recipes for the sealed flask: Rough shatters it in the intake
    tray (big instant dose). Coarse pops the stopper. Fine reseats the
    stopper. Very Fine turns the flask into a radiation-hardened Level 5
    key. 1:1 gives you an ordinary empty beaker.

    system (already had the growing-mould code and IHzIrradiate), plus
    Injuries/Bloodloss/Infect, PointLight and CreateItem.

 SCP-173-B - "Practice Sculpture" (room2173cages, Indev Zone)

 Memory meter: Alzheimer's disease (left, above the stress bar)
  - The Class-D has Alzheimer's disease. A lavender bar with a brain icon
    and the current stage number (IndevMemory.bb). It starts at 10 and
    rises slowly, about a point every 50 seconds, twice as fast while
    stress is 70 or more. Each new stage is announced.
  - The 7 stages, with small symptoms spaced minutes apart:
      1  (0-14)   no impairment
      2  (15-29)  forgetting a word, where you put something
      3  (30-44)  lost in familiar corridors, forgetting new names, focus
      4  (45-59)  forgetting recent events, repeating the same thought,
                  numbers swim (keypads), denial
      5  (60-74)  "where is this, what day is it", inventory items
                  misplaced in odd slots
      6  (75-89)  delusions (things stolen), fear and mood swings,
                  wandering (the view turns), not recognising people
      7  (90-100) the body forgets: stamina capped at 50 and drains
                  faster, stumbles
  - Preserved skills: while you read a document the decline pauses.
  - Medicine: an ordinary pill slows the decline for five minutes.
    SCP-500 cures it: the bar drops to zero and stops rising.
  - Test switch: -testmemory <0-100> with -roomtest.

 Stress meter (left, above the blink bar)
  - Builds from:
      - SCP-173 in view;
      - SCP-106 nearby;
      - SCP-096 hunting;
      - SCP-718's stare;
      - SCP-895;
      - lost sanity;
      - bad wounds.
  - Drains when things are quiet, faster while crouching.
  - Effects are subtle: a heartbeat at 40+, brief blur and tremors at
    60+, rare intrusive thoughts at 80+.
  - SCP-122, CF-1 glasses, soup and candy all calm it.

 Improved blinking
  - How fast you blink depends on your condition: stress, exhaustion,
    blood loss, injuries, or calm crouching.
  - SCP effects can change it for a while: SCP-718, SCP-096, SCP-719,
    pills, MJOLNIR and SCP-693.

 Armour bar (bottom-right)

  - Hidden while a held item uses that corner.

  - Radiation - accumulated exposure. Fades slowly.
  - Geiger - radiation detected right now. Flickers and clicks.
  - Infection - SCP-008, SCP-214/217 and SCP-718 blisters.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6a. DESIGN PILLARS (Design.md)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Written down at the repo root as Design.md. Enforced on every new
  addition from 2026-09-23 on:

  1. Every pill has a reason to exist.
       Purpose, meter-visible effect, a drawback when the effect is
       strong, a reason to carry it over a first-aid kit, and a
       logical spawn site. Audit on 2026-09-23: every existing pill
       already meets the bar.

  2. New content brings its own lore up front.
       One document per new pill; two per new SCP; three per new
       event or zone. Each carries a believable reason it exists, a
       specific Foundation connection, and a gameplay purpose.
       Environmental storytelling (stains, notes, chalked messages)
       is welcome. Writing lore afterwards to explain content that's
       already there is not.

  3. Vanilla-first.
       Expand existing SCP:CB systems before inventing a new one.
       Reuse Sanity, Injuries, Bloodloss, Stamina, the four wearable
       slots, the meter HUD, card readers and keypads, and the Msg
       system before adding new globals, HUD regions or lock types.
       Recent examples: the SCP-914 refinement fallback (extends the
       existing SCP-914), the Indev-Zone Level 5 card reader (reuses
       CreateDoor keycard levels) and the Christmas music (reuses
       StreamSound_Strict from the existing music channel).


 6. FIXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Bug fixes: game-breaking issues resolved without altering the
    original designs -
      - Unbeatable seeds: layouts are validated on generation and
        regenerated if the exit can't be reached from the start

      - Overlapping rooms: OverlapResolver.bb now catches rooms

        moved. Rooms flagged 'disable overlap check' are left alone.

  - New documents no longer overlap props in rooms.

  - SCP-718's document is placed correctly in both room variants.
  - Saving and loading no longer breaks SCP-993, SCP-719 and the other new
    SCP props: quick-loading keeps them working.
  - Clicking works reliably on every new SCP: TVs, SCP-718, SCP-330,

  - SCP-993's black screen can't get stuck any more.
  - SCP-1074: the pull no longer walks you backwards, and you can look

  - room2newroom65: the player no longer falls through the lower level's

  - On-screen messages wider than the screen now wrap onto several lines.

- The projector is built in code after the reference photo (gunmetal body, chrome plates, lens barrel with a gemstone-layered lens, two reels on arms that turn while it runs, knobs, crank).
